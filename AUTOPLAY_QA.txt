OHGS v41 — AUTOMATIC VIDEO PLAYBACK QA
Date: 2 November 2026
Release: v41-visible-video-autoplay

What changed
- Visible previews request playback immediately, muted and inline, with looping enabled. The browser can buffer while fulfilling the play request instead of waiting for canplay before requesting playback.
- Network speed estimates no longer prevent autoplay. The optimized mobile MP4 is used for every automatic preview. Data Saver, reduced-motion preferences, offline state and browser autoplay restrictions retain the Play control.
- A preview becomes eligible when at least 30% visible. The existing 5% exit threshold and 450ms grace period avoid interruptions from small scroll movements.
- Using Play, native controls or Sound on no longer turns off autoplay for the whole page. After that video leaves the screen, the next visible preview starts muted automatically.
- An explicitly paused video stays paused. Returning to a video that was paused automatically resumes its saved position. Only one video plays at a time and offscreen sources are not loaded in advance.
- Existing timing protections, bilingual controls, source fallback, buffering feedback, saved themes and responsive layout are retained.
- Release keys, deployment checks and upload instructions are updated to v41.
