OHGS — SIMPLE GOOGLE SEARCH SETUP

The website code is prepared for Google, but Google decides when to crawl and rank it. After the new version is live:

1. Open https://search.google.com/search-console and add the Domain property: ohgs.co.tz
2. Copy the TXT verification record Google gives you into the DNS provider that manages ohgs.co.tz.
3. After verification, open Sitemaps and submit: https://ohgs.co.tz/sitemap.xml
4. Open URL Inspection, inspect https://ohgs.co.tz/ and choose Request indexing.
5. Also request indexing for /products, /contact and the most important product pages.
6. Keep the business name, phone number and Uhuru Road address consistent on the website, Google Business Profile and social pages.
7. Add new products and real photos regularly. Do not copy descriptions from other websites.

Useful live checks:
- https://ohgs.co.tz/robots.txt
- https://ohgs.co.tz/sitemap.xml
- https://ohgs.co.tz/version-check.txt

Google indexing is not instant. Search Console will show crawl and indexing progress after deployment.
