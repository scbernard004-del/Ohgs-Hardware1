OHGS v35 — IMAGE ALIGNMENT AND SCROLLING HEADER QA
Date: 20 October 2026

Fixes in this release
- All 93 product-card, product-detail and service image positions now have a stable 4:3 frame in the HTML. Pictures cannot stretch the frame from their intrinsic portrait height, and alignment works before JavaScript runs.
- Home's primary video and two preview cards share aligned outer top and bottom edges. The full video remains visible during playback.
- Header changes from 76 to 64 pixels on desktop and 68 to 60 pixels on mobile/tablet. Logo and wordmark shrink with it; theme, menu and language controls retain their touch sizes.
- Separate scroll thresholds prevent flickering near the top. Header returns to full size and removes its shadow at the top. The mobile menu follows the current header height.
- Phone-status borders are excluded from the INGCO compactor and cut-off-saw display areas without changing the original product photos or their labels.
- Removed a phone-status banner from About. Workshop now shows an air compressor matching its product heading. Corrected Gallery's trolley label and removed its duplicate tile.
- Each video shows one clear Play button before loading and native controls during playback.
- All pages use the new v37-loading-feedback cache key.

Earlier improvements retained
- One coherent stylesheet replaces the overlapping legacy design styles on every public page.
- Stable header with visible logo, 44-pixel theme/menu controls and readable language selector. Mobile ordering remains in the bottom dock.
- Text appears before the Home videos. Compact portrait videos preserve the full frame. Product pictures use contain sizing so labels and equipment are not cropped.
- Native responsive WebP images, intrinsic dimensions and lazy loading. Below-fold video posters load near the screen. Video files start downloading only after Play is selected.
- Lower-bandwidth mobile video files. All MP4s use fast-start metadata. The new campaign mobile file is about 81% smaller than the original upload.
- The 14-second Build with us video is included on Home, Gallery, Services, Projects, Construction Materials and the Road Cutter page.
- The video contains a construction campaign and OHGS branding. It does not establish a sale price, machine model, rental service or stock. The site adds a project-supply enquiry rather than making those unsupported claims.
- Eleven product links now open matching product detail pages. Product search works in English and Kiswahili; WhatsApp prefilled requests switch language too.
- Replaced an empty Gallery video poster and regenerated two empty optimized image files.

Verification limits
DOM tests check interactions and source logic; they do not render browser screenshots or prove layout on a physical phone. The current environment has no supported local preview browser. SMTP delivery is not tested against a live email account.
